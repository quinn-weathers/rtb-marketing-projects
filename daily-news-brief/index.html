Moved ▶ DOJ / FBI · 28 CFR 25 · effective 11/27 Heat72▲ FR 2026-21920 · full text read 10/28

Read in full: the dealer “firearm handler check” rule is voluntary, needs the worker’s written consent, and covers staff with access to guns or ammunition

We read the full rule text (17 Federal Register pages) through the FR API. Key points: licensed dealers may run checks but are not required to; an “eligible employee” is one whose duties give access to firearms, ammunition or the materials and tools to make them; the dealer must give the employee notice and obtain informed, written consent first (34 U.S.C. 40901); state “point of contact” agencies do not have to process these checks unless they choose to; the rule imposes nothing on state or local governments. It takes effect 27 November (T−30). Coverage beyond NSSF: one Daily Caller piece (10/27) framing it as easier hiring for store owners.

RTB angleNow safe to explain plainly. A short “for shop owners and staff” note: voluntary, consent-based, starts 27 November. Link to the rule, not a summary of a summary. Not legal advice.
