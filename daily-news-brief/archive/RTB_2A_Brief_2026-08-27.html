The roughly ten states with assault-weapons bans now have a Supreme Court question presented pointed at them — and the features question is the one that decides whether a ruling means anything

New framing this run, drawn from a source read in full for the first time. SAF’s Kostas Moros, writing on USCCA’s blog on 08/22, states that AR-15-style bans exist in roughly ten states and describes the mechanism most of them use: Cook County, Illinois bans certain firearms by name — AR-15s and AK-47s — and restricts semi-automatic rifles that accept magazines over ten rounds if they carry features such as a pistol grip, folding or telescoping stock, muzzle brake, or compensator. Handgun rosters: California requires features rather than banning them, a mirror-image version of the same question.

Defense Distributed v. Blanche — the as-applied correction holds, no notice of appeal, and the ruling date is still unresolved across three accounts

No movement in twenty-four hours, and the correction issued yesterday stands. The relief is “as applied to Defense Distributed, SAF, and covered members,” and AmmoLand states directly that “the ruling is significant, but it is not nationwide.” That is party-scoped relief — the same architecture as the Silencer Shop injunction — not universal vacatur. The holdings are unchanged: on the Fifth Amendment, the rule is void for vagueness because it “does not provide fair notice to the reader about when a component becomes subject to enforcement”; on the Second, applying Bruen, the government’s colonial barrel-proofing and gunpowder-storage analogues fail and the rule “does not merely lack supporting analogues. It contradicts the actual historical tradition of personal gunsmithing.” The case continues from Bondi v. VanDerStok, where a 7–2 Supreme Court majority upheld the same rule against APA claims, with Justice Gorsuch noting the holding was “limited.” The ruling date remains unresolved: The Reload says “Monday” (08/17), the SAF-hosted PDF is filenamed 8.19.26, AmmoLand posted 08/23 without one.

RTB angleThe corrected framing is now the settled framing — check any draft written before yesterday.
