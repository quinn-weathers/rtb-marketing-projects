Congress returns to a docket the courts, the executive branch and the data filled while it was out

Both chambers are scheduled back today. congress.gov was unreachable from this environment for a seventeenth consecutive run, so the return date remains carried, not re-verified. What is verifiable is the ledger the recess produced without a single legislative act: the Justice Department filed a Supreme Court brief arguing a class of rifle bans is unconstitutional, the Court set a December argument date on the same question, 27 state attorneys general filed on the same side, the President issued pardons in two National Firearms Act prosecutions, a federal appellate panel put registration requirements outside the Second Amendment, another left a state surveillance mandate standing, a federal district court held a state waiting period unconstitutional as to two named people, and the federal background-check data recorded the largest NFA volume shift this board has logged. The standing vehicles — national reciprocity, the Hearing Protection Act, the nationwide constitutional-carry bill — have not moved, and the 47-lawmaker August letter asking the Justice Department to stop defending the zero-tax NFA registration scheme is still publicly unanswered. Johnson v. Jacobson — the carry-reciprocity petition, still the question members actually ask

A Georgia long-haul driver holding valid Georgia and Florida carry licences asks whether Minnesota may require him to unload and case his handgun at the state line because it recognises neither. The Eighth Circuit called reciprocity a matter of “convenience” grounded in interstate comity and compared carry permits to professional licences. The petition leans on Wolford v. Lopez and its holding that the Second Amendment has the “same meaning in all parts” of the country. The Court has not agreed to hear it, it is not on the September 28 conference list, and there has been no docket movement and no second outlet in five days.

RTB angleThis is the highest-intent search topic on the board and the one where a wrong sentence does the most damage. Never tell a member whether a specific state honours their permit. Point them to the issuing authority and the destination authority, and state plainly that reciprocity changes without notice and often without publicity — which is the honest case for having counsel on retainer before a trip, made without a threat. Describe the petition as a petition every single time. The Eighth Circuit’s professional-licence comparison is quotable as the court’s own reasoning; do not characterise the panel.

The 2026 National Firearms Survey — four numbers, four meanings, never interchangeable

Fully reconciled and unchanged in 24 hours: 40 million AR-15-type rifles; 60 million including other similarly styled semi-automatic rifles; 26.4 million adults owning at least one of the two categories; 461 million firearms overall, comprising roughly 208 million handguns, 154 million rifles and 99 million shotguns. RTB angleThis is the strongest publishable dataset RTB has this year and the easiest one to misuse.
